Study Buddy

Flashcards for Years 1–4, made for home.

A parent sets up the family. Each child picks their name and practises maths, reading, and Year 4 vocabulary — including this week’s words, last week, and older weeks.

For parents

  1. Open Study Buddy in the browser and set a four-digit family PIN.
  2. Add each child: first name, school year (1–4), and an avatar.
  3. Shared decks are already there for Year 1–2 Maths and Reading, and Year 4 Vocabulary.
  4. Open a year deck to add or edit cards. On Year 4 Vocabulary, use Add words to paste a word card from XL Education (word, part of speech, level, definitions, examples).

Children never type the PIN after the device is unlocked. They only tap their name. Keep the PIN for grown-ups.

For children

  1. Tap your name on the home screen.
  2. Open a deck and choose Study or Cards.
  3. Flip the card, then tap I know this or Still learning.
  4. For vocabulary, open Weekly words to revise or quiz this week and previous weeks.

Year 4 vocabulary

Word cards show the front first. Tap a word to see definitions, synonyms, antonyms, and examples. Letter tabs and search help you jump through the list like a notebook.

Every word stores the day it was added, so weekly revise and quiz stay organised.

Weekly words

Words are grouped from Sunday to Saturday. A word added on any of those days counts for that week. You will see:

  • This week — new words
  • Last week — last Sunday to Saturday
  • Older weeks — anything before that

Tap a word to read it. Use Revise this week to flip cards, or Quiz this week for a short multiple-choice quiz.

Using it on more than one device

The live app is the Vercel website (not this GitHub page). On each tablet or computer, open that site and enter the family PIN once. After that, children pick their name. Words saved in the shared database appear on every signed-in device.

If the home screen says the copy is “this browser only”, the shared database is not connected. A parent needs to check Vercel: both DATABASE_URL and SESSION_SECRET on Production, then redeploy.
